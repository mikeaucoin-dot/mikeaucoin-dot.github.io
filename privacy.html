Privacy policy

Your account. Your private computer.

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy describes Zedwork Private Backup Verification, a personal, owner-managed desktop application, and this application-information website.

Google data accessed

After the owner authorizes a Google account, enabled features can read selected or linked Drive file contents and metadata, calendar lists, and events in selected calendars. The application can create or update its managed calendar entries and reminders in the calendar selected by the owner.

The authorization screen describes the Google permissions requested. Some API permissions technically cover more files or calendar events than the application's normal selected-document and managed-event workflows use.

How data is used and stored

Google data is used for the owner's document reading, backup checks, private household calendar view, school-date organization and reminders. Working records, downloaded originals, document versions, local checklist history and local backups are stored on the owner's private computer. Credentials saved by the desktop application are protected with Windows data protection. Browser connections use separate local browser profiles.

Calendar entries synchronized by the application are stored in the owner's Google Calendar. If the owner separately enables copying backups into a Google Drive for desktop folder, Google Drive for desktop handles the cloud upload under that service's own settings.

Sharing and limited use

The application does not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models. Its scheduled collection and processing run locally without an AI service. Separate actions the owner chooses to take with other tools are controlled by the owner and those tools' terms.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and owner controls

The owner controls local retention. Original notices, versions and backups may remain after an item is marked handled; marking an item handled is not a request to erase history. Removing local application data does not automatically erase copies in local backups or entries in Google Calendar.

The owner can revoke the application's Google access through Google Account's third-party connections controls. Revocation stops future authorized API access; it does not delete data already saved locally or calendar entries already synchronized. The owner manages deletion of those records and backups separately.

This information website

This static site does not collect Google authorizations, passwords, school records, calendar contents or backups. It uses no application analytics, advertising, tracking cookies or contact forms. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection logs under its own privacy policy.

Contact and changes

This application is managed by its owner for personal use. Contact the owner through the user-support email displayed on Google's consent screen. This policy will be updated when the application's relevant data practices change.
